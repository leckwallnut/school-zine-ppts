# Copyable teaching layouts

Open [the visual index](layout-index.jpg) and match its numbers to slides in [layout-library.pptx](../assets/layout-library.pptx). In PowerPoint, copy the slide thumbnail into your deck and select **Keep Source Formatting**. Edit the example text, speaker notes and images in your copy. Use **Change Picture** or replace a picture in the Selection Pane when a new illustration is needed.

| Slide | Layout | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Cover | Lesson title and a short purpose line. |
| 02 | Section break | A change of pace before a new task or answer. |
| 03 | Scene with text on the right | A short explanation beside a contextual illustration. |
| 04 | Full-width reading | A substantial passage and one reading question. |
| 05 | Annotated passage | A marked phrase and an explanation in the margin. |
| 06 | Illustrated scenario | A visual case with the situation and decision beside it. |
| 07 | Side-by-side comparison | Two examples under the same question. |
| 08 | Worked example | A complete response broken down by function. |
| 09 | Process diagram | Three editable stages joined by native arrow shapes. |
| 10 | Pair discussion | A prompt inside a speech bubble beside an activity cutout. |
| 11 | Independent writing | Three steps on a quiet dark writing surface. |
| 12 | Answer reveal | A model answer and rationale for the slide after a prompt. |
| 13 | Exit ticket | Two short response tasks on a taped paper component. |
| 14 | Data table | A native table, with space for a question beneath it. |
| 15 | Partner revision | A step-by-step routine with a right-side activity cutout. |
| 16 | Image with caption | A large scenario image and an editable caption strip. |
| 17 | Quotation and response | A short quotation, attribution and response prompt. |
| 18 | Sort and classify | Three rows for discussion, classification or a debrief. |

For layouts 10, 13 and 16, the picture and its text box are separate objects. Select the text box to edit wording. When resizing the art, apply the catalog’s text region to the fitted image and reposition the text. Keep the question and model answer on adjacent slides when using layout 12 as a reveal.
