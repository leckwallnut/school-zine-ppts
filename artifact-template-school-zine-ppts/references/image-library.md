The catalog records source, dimensions, alpha and SHA-256. Finish the Line pack folders also retain their own manifests, prompts and sample rounds where supplied. Use each original pack’s situation as a source when you adapt it.

For text placement, apply `x, y, width, height` as fractions of the fitted PNG’s complete rectangle, including transparent margins. Use the measured regions in the catalog. For the two-column spread use the heading band and separate text fields. For the caption strip, use one short line. Keep type above the speech bubble’s halftones and away from the notebook’s tape and binding.

Place the teacher’s photo behind the photo-corner frame and use its catalogued image region to keep the subject clear of the corner mounts.

Choose a small prop from Finish the Line pack 03 when it helps signal a task. Choose one activity cutout for an interaction, then keep the task itself in editable text. Use scenario illustrations with the whole composition visible when students need details from it. ImageGen illustrations are teaching cues. Cite documentary source material separately when the lesson requires evidence.

For the illustrated settings in batch 05, use the measured blank field and its recommended text color. The batch 06 props can cue a source, measurement, or investigation task alongside the teacher’s editable material.

Use the batch 07 science, maths, and technology vignettes to introduce an activity. Add equations, measurements, and circuit or geometry diagrams as editable slide objects. Batch 08 includes adult and mixed-age groups for discussions, interviews, demonstrations, and rehearsals.

Batch 09 contains two ordered three-frame sequences, The Borrowed Room and The Changing Stream, plus four standalone scenes. Use the catalog’s sequence and frame fields to keep the images in order, or hold back the last frame for a prediction task. Place editable prompts and captions beside or below the full-width pictures.

Batch 10 provides blank definition, formula, evidence, comparison, sequence and checklist boxes, plus labels for short text. Place a separate editable text box in each marked region. Use cream lettering in the indigo formula box and indigo on the lighter components. Batch 11 provides straight, curved, two-way and branching arrows, three star markers, a brace for grouping, a swash for highlighting, and a transparent-center ring. Keep the arrow orientation and placement consistent with the relationships in the lesson.

When extending the library, add a numbered batch under `assets/art/new/` and append records to the same catalog. Keep existing filenames and IDs stable. Record prompts, provenance, dimensions, alpha, SHA-256 and measured text regions for new surfaces and components. Proof the batch on both base colors.
