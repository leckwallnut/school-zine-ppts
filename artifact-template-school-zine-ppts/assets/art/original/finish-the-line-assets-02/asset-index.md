# Finish the Line · Art pack 02

Ten illustrations for TeachVault’s team writing and speaking game. The art matches the indigo, marigold, coral, and mint palette of pack 01.

## Widescreen transition

- [Surprise Reveal](backgrounds/surprise-reveal.jpg). Show it between locking the endings and displaying the scenario. The quieter left side can hold the transition text.

## Scenario reveals

- [The Cardboard Arcade](scenarios/cardboard-arcade.jpg). A student tilts a handmade cardboard arcade game as a classmate watches a marble roll through the maze.
- [The Book Drive](scenarios/book-drive.jpg). Three classmates approach a student crouching beside two boxes of books outside the library.
- [The Greenhouse Door](scenarios/tiny-greenhouse-door.jpg). A student with a watering can spots a tiny mint door and a trail of muddy footprints at the base of a large planter.
- [The Trophy Case Duck](scenarios/trophy-case-duck.jpg). A student in mint glasses crouches beside a yellow rubber duck beneath a glass case full of trophies.
- [The Wrong Group Chat](scenarios/wrong-group-chat.jpg). Two friends on a school bench look at a phone showing colored chat bubbles.
- [The Last Window Seat](scenarios/last-seat.jpg). Two students pause at the last open window seat on a school bus.
- [Tortoise on the Move](scenarios/tortoise-on-the-move.jpg). Two students watch a small tortoise cross the hallway near an open library door.

The seven editable rounds in `sample-rounds.json` match these images by ID. Show only the opener while teams write. After they commit, show the image and situation. Give one point for completing the thought and one for explaining how the ending fits or clashes with a specific detail from the scene.

## Transparent cutouts

- [Context Magnifier](props/context-magnifier.png). Cue to cite a specific scene detail when explaining an ending.
- [Traditional Ending Book](props/traditional-ending-book.png). Cue to reveal a proverb’s traditional ending after discussion.

The PNGs were made with the built-in ImageGen tool. The manifest includes alt text and relative paths. See `image-prompts.txt` for the generation prompts.
