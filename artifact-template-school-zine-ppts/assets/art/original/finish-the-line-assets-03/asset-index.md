# Finish the Line · Art pack 03

Ten reusable transparent PNG cutouts in the same indigo, marigold, coral, mint, and cream school-zine palette as packs 01 and 02.

- [Writing Pencil](props/writing-pencil.png). Writing phase, answer entry, or writing prompts.
- [Round Hourglass](props/round-hourglass.png). Writing timer, thinking time, or a brief pause.
- [Discussion Bubbles](props/discussion-bubbles.png). Pair discussion, team planning, or a class debrief.
- [Open Padlock](props/reveal-padlock.png). Reveal a scenario, unlock a clue, or show an answer.
- [Hint Light Bulb](props/hint-light-bulb.png). Optional hints, examples, or vocabulary support.
- [Detail Highlighter](props/detail-highlighter.png). Point out a specific detail or emphasize a word.
- [Linked Paper Clips](props/linked-paper-clips.png). Connect an ending to a detail or group related ideas.
- [Team Pennant](props/team-pennant.png). Team setup, team labels, or active team display.
- [Score Star](props/score-star.png). Show earned points or celebrate a completed round.
- [Next Turn Arrow](props/next-turn-arrow.png). Pass the turn, move to the next team, or start a new round.

The artwork has no embedded labels, team names, or point values. Pair it with editable text for the action or stage it represents. The asset manifest includes relative paths and alt text.

The illustrations were created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The generation prompts are in `image-prompts.txt`.
