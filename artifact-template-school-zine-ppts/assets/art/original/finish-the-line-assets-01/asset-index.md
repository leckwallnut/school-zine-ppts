# Finish the Line · Art pack 01

Ten illustrations for TeachVault’s team writing and speaking game.

## Widescreen screens

- [Finish the Line cover](backgrounds/finish-the-line-cover.jpg). Catalog and game opening.
- [Writing Desk](backgrounds/writing-desk.jpg). Writing phase and timer.
- [Team Scoreboard](backgrounds/team-scoreboard.jpg). Round recap and final scores.

The left side of each widescreen background has room for a title, prompt, controls, or scoreboard.

## Scenario reveals

- [Presentation Glitch](scenarios/presentation-glitch.jpg). Three students look worried over a laptop while holding a printed outline and a phone with a picture.
- [The Paper Bridge](scenarios/paper-bridge.jpg). A folded paper bridge sags under a blue weight as two students lean over the desk.
- [Credit for the Comic](scenarios/comic-credit.jpg). Two students share a tense look in front of their comic about a pigeon and a skateboard.
- [The Rained-Out Fair](scenarios/courtyard-rainout.jpg). Two students watch rain hit the colorful flags and empty stalls of the school fair.
- [Just Before the Lights](scenarios/stage-blackout.jpg). Two students pause in the wings beside a red curtain and look toward each other as the stage goes dark.

The editable round text in `sample-rounds.json` uses the same IDs as the scenario art. Show only the opener while teams write. Once every team has committed, show the scenario image, situation and discussion prompt together. For the proverb round, the traditional ending and its meaning can appear after the team explanations.

## Transparent cutouts

- [Committed Answer](props/committed-answer.png). Cue that written endings are locked.
- [Team Microphone](props/team-microphone.png). Cue to read and explain an ending.

Award one point for an ending that completes the thought and one for explaining the fit or mismatch using a specific detail from the situation.

The pictures are PNGs created with ImageGen. The manifest includes relative paths and alt text. See `image-prompts.txt` for the generation prompts.
